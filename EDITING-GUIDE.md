# Editing your site

Your site's words, roles, experience, recommendations and resume all live in one content file. You edit them in Pages CMS, a free editor. When you press Save, your site updates in about a minute.

## Sign in

1. Open the invitation email from Pages CMS and follow its link to sign in. You don't need a GitHub account.
2. Open **robin-portfolio**. Check that the top left says **main**. That's your live site.
3. Click **Website content**. Each section of the page has its own panel, top to bottom.

## Change words

Edit the text in any panel, then press **Save** at the top right. The live site updates in about a minute. Refresh your site to see it.

- **Lists** (captions, roles, accomplishments, tools): **Add an item** adds a line. The trash icon removes one. Drag the dotted handle on the left to reorder.
- **Highlights, jobs, principles and recommendations** fold up to a short title. Click the arrow to open one, or use **Expand all**.

## Replace your resume

Scroll to **Contact and resume**. Under **Resume PDF**, click the trash icon to remove the current file, upload the new PDF, then press **Save**. Both resume buttons on the site point to whatever file is chosen here.

Keep your phone number off the public copy. Bots collect phone numbers from public PDFs.

## Recommendations

Open **Recommendations**. Each one has a quote, a name, their role, and how they know you.

- The first three with **Show on the site** switched on appear on the page, in that order. Drag to reorder.
- To retire one without deleting it, switch **Show on the site** off.
- Only post a recommendation once the person has said yes to having it on your site.

## Undo a mistake

- **Small slip:** put the old text back and press **Save**.
- **Need an earlier version:** the clock button next to **Save** shows past versions of the content, so you can see what it said before.
- **Something looks broken:** tell Phil. He can roll the whole site back to the last good version in one click.

## Good to know

- Every Save publishes. If you're making several edits, make them all, then press Save once.
- The look, the animation and the layout aren't in the editor, so they can't break by accident. Ask Phil for design changes.
- The headline holds its place best when it stays short. Under six words works best.
