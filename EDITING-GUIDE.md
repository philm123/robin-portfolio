# Editing your site

Your site's words, roles, experience, recommendations and resume all live in one content file. You edit them in Pages CMS, a free editor that saves straight to your site's GitHub repository. Netlify sees each save and republishes the site in about a minute.

## Sign in

1. Go to **app.pagescms.org** and choose **Sign in with GitHub**.
2. Pick the **robin-hoffman-site** repository.
3. Open **Website content**. Every section of the page has its own panel.

## Change words

Open the panel for the section (Top of page, Target roles, Experience, and so on), edit the text, then press **Save**. The live site updates in about a minute.

- **Lists** (roles, accomplishments, tools): use **Add** to add a line, the trash icon to remove one, and drag to reorder.
- **Jobs and recommendations** collapse to their name. Click one to open it.

## Replace your resume

Open **Contact and resume**. Under **Resume PDF**, upload the new PDF and press **Save**. Both resume buttons on the site point to whatever file is chosen here.

Keep your phone number off the public copy. Bots collect phone numbers from public PDFs.

## Recommendations

Open **Recommendations**. Each one has a quote, name, role and how they know you.

- The first three with **Show on the site** switched on appear on the page, in the order listed.
- To retire one without deleting it, switch **Show on the site** off.
- Only post a recommendation once the person has said yes to having it on your site.

## Undo a mistake

**Fastest:** in Netlify, open the site, go to **Deploys**, click the last good version and choose **Publish deploy**. The site goes back to it right away, at no cost. Your next save in Pages CMS publishes again from the current content, so fix the content as well.

**Exact:** every save is kept in GitHub history. Phil can restore any earlier version of the content file in a couple of minutes.

## Good to know

- Each save publishes the site, and each publish uses 15 of the 300 free Netlify credits a month. That's about 20 saves a month. If you're making several edits, make them all, then save once.
- The look, the animation and the layout aren't in the editor. That keeps them from breaking by accident. Ask Phil for design changes.
- The headline keeps its place only if it stays short. Under six words works best.
